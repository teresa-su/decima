    How DECIMA works¶

    DECIMA infers θ = ([Fe/H]_init, [α/Fe]_init, log age, EEP) for each star, plus its distance and extinction. θ lives on a grid of MIST v2.5 isochrones (v/v_crit = 0.4). Two neural posterior estimators (NPEs) and one density estimator, all trained on simulations from that grid, give a first posterior over θ. Importance sampling then refines it with the photometry and parallax, and adds the posteriors over distance and extinction.

    The details, derivations and validation are in the DECIMA paper (in preparation). This page is the short version.

    The pipeline at a glance¶

    flowchart TD

    subgraph S1["Stage 1: distance-independent, neural posterior estimation"]
        direction TB
        A["1. Draw n_latent_x noise-free spectroscopic observables s′<br/>around the measured s, weighted by q(s′)"]
        B["2. For each s′ draw n_theta values of θ from a defensive mixture<br/>mixture_alpha: q(θ | s′, M<sub>pseudo,K</sub>) · rest: q(θ | s′)"]
        C["3. Stage-1 weight: q(s′) · q(θ | s′) / mixture proposal<br/>(the parallax is counted once, in stage 2)"]
        A --> B --> C
    end

    subgraph S2["Stage 2: importance sampling with the photometry and parallax"]
        direction TB
        D["4. Isochrone lookup: absolute magnitudes M<sub>b</sub>(θ) of every draw"]
        E["5. Fit distance modulus μ and extinction E to m<sub>b</sub> − M<sub>b</sub><br/>(weighted least squares; the parallax joins when precise)"]
        F["6. Draw (μ, E) around the fit with E ≥ 0; convert μ to distance d"]
        G["7. Stage-2 weight: photometric and parallax likelihoods,<br/>IMF and distance priors, (log g) / proposal density"]
        D --> E --> F --> G
    end

    OUT["8. Multiply the weights and resample (θ, d, E):<br/>unweighted posterior samples, percentiles and diagnostics"]
    IN --> A
    C --> D
    G --> OUT

    Settings that act on each step are named in the settings reference.

    Inputs¶

    Symbol What
    s = ([Fe/H], [α/Fe], log T_eff) spectroscopic surface observables
    M_pseudo,K = ϖ · 10^(0.2 Ks − 2) the pseudo absolute magnitude (parallax ϖ in mas), equal to 10^(−0.2 M_Ks) for a star without extinction
    m_b, b ∈ {G, BP, RP, J, H, Ks} apparent magnitudes
    ϖ parallax
    log g optional (use_logg)

    The spectroscopy and M_pseudo,K are distance-independent; the magnitudes and parallax are not.

    The trained models¶

    Model What it is
    q(θ | s, M_pseudo,K) NPE conditioned on the spectroscopy and the pseudo absolute magnitude
    q(θ | s) NPE conditioned on the spectroscopy only
    q(s) density of the spectroscopic observables under the training prior

    All three were trained with sbi on 70,000 noise-free simulations drawn uniformly over the grid. The NPEs work in a transformed θ, rescaled to the grid range and logit-transformed (decima.theta). DECIMA converts their draws back to physical θ.

    Stage 1: spectroscopic posterior¶

    Measurement noise is handled at inference time, not in the networks: 1. For each star, DECIMA draws n_latent_x noise-free versions of the observables around the measured ones, and weights each by q(s). 2. For each of these, it draws n_theta values of θ from a defensive mixture: a fraction mixture_alpha (0.1) from the luminosity-informed q(θ | s, M_pseudo,K), the rest from q(θ | s).

    The mixture concentrates draws where the parallax will put the star, while still covering the tails of the posterior.

    Stage 2: photometry and parallax¶

    For every stage-1 draw, the isochrone gives the absolute magnitudes: 1. DECIMA fits a distance modulus and an extinction E to the observed magnitudes, and draws (d, E) around the fit, with E ≥ 0. 2. Each draw is weighted by the photometric likelihood, the parallax likelihood, the distance prior, the IMF prior and, optionally, the log g likelihood. 3. The draws are resampled in proportion to their weights.

    The weights also swap the proposal for q(θ | s), so the parallax is counted once.

    Priors¶

    The trained models are deliberately kept free of physical priors: they see θ drawn uniformly over the grid. Priors are applied afterwards, as importance weights in stage 2. DECIMA currently has the ability to apply the IMF prior on θ and a distance prior; support for user-defined priors is planned.

    On Prior
    θ uniform over the grid in training; the IMF (Kroupa 2001) is applied in stage 2 (imf_prior)
    distance Bailer-Jones+21 generalised gamma, with parameters from the star's HEALPix pixel (per-star L, alpha, beta override the map)
    extinction flat, E ≥ 0
